Photorealistic laboratory fluorescence microscopy macro capture of the abaxial epidermis of a Zea mays corn leaf. On one side, dumbbell-shaped stomatal guard cells are turgid and wide open, exposing the clear stomatal pore with visible chloroplasts glowing in vivid green inside the cells. On the opposing side under water deficit conditions, the guard cells appear flaccid and tightly compressed, fully closing the aperture. Extremely sharp cellular texture of undulating epidermal cell walls, high magnification with vivid botanical contrast and laboratory depth of field.
