Extreme macro photography of a cluster of flat, overlapping, fish-scale-like insect egg masses laid on the underside of a vibrant green corn leaf (Zea mays). A tiny, translucent pale cream neonate borer caterpillar with a dark glossy head capsule is actively chewing its way out of the eggshell. Deep depth of field highlighting the fine parallel veins and crystalline micro-hairs of the maize leaf epidermis under crisp diffused laboratory lighting.
